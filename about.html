About

Built for
manufacturing

Cryonic Systems develops manufacturing execution and operational intelligence software for industrial manufacturers. The company is based in Michigan and serves discrete and batch production operations.

The products come out of daily work inside automotive production environments, and they target the gaps that established MES platforms leave open: operator screens designed around the database rather than the station, visibility that lags the line, OEE reported as a single plant-wide number, and traceability that breaks down at the moment a containment starts.

Cryonic MES addresses each of those directly. Software is deployed on-premise on customer hardware, production data stays inside the plant network, and support is handled by the engineers who build the product.
