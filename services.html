Services

Implementation, integration
and long-term support

Deployment and ongoing support for Cryonic MES and Axiom in industrial production environments. Work is handled by the engineers who build the software.

Deployment & Commissioning

Installation on plant hardware, database setup, environment configuration and commissioning alongside plant IT and controls staff — including validation on live lines before cutover.

Machine & OPC Integration

Tag mapping, station handshake definition and interface scripting between the equipment layer and the MES, documented so controls engineers can maintain it afterward.

Customer EDI Onboarding

Setup and validation of inbound customer transactions — planning schedules, purchase orders and changes, shipping schedules, production sequence releases and warehouse orders.

ERP Integration

Connection between production data and the business system, including SAP advance ship notice output, so shipping events reach the ERP without manual re-entry.

Legacy MES Migration

Structured transition from an existing platform: part and BOM data migration, parallel running during changeover, and a cutover plan that does not require stopping production.

Support & Maintenance

Direct support without a tier-one queue. Updates, configuration changes and issue resolution are handled by the people who wrote the code and understand the line.
