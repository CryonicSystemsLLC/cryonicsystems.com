Why Cryonic MES

Engineered where legacy
systems fall short

Each capability targets a specific, well-known failure point in existing manufacturing execution platforms.

Operator-First Interface

Screens designed around the station rather than the database. Plain-language status, large touch targets and one clear action per screen reduce mis-scans and shorten operator training.

Real-Time Visibility

OPC UA connectivity pushes cycle counts, machine states and faults to every open screen as they happen. The floor and the front office see the same data at the same moment.

Self-Service Configuration

Work centers, production lines, parts, BOMs, production stages, OPC tags and print queues are configured through the interface. Adding a part number is an afternoon task, not a vendor ticket.

Long-Term Maintainability

Predictable data structures, documented machine interfaces and a clean separation between production control and business operations keep the system serviceable years after go-live.

Station-Level OEE

Availability, performance and quality calculated per line and per station with downtime reasons attached. Losses resolve to a specific location and cause instead of a plant-wide average.

Serial-Level Genealogy

Every part receives a unique serial at creation, carrying its material consumption, station history, container and shipment. Containment scopes to affected parts rather than an entire shift.
